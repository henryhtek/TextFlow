Form Auto-Populate

Create profile snippets that TextFlow uses to fill common form fields.

How it works

Press Ctrl+Shift+F on any page with a form. TextFlow detects common fields and fills empty ones from matching snippets.

Recommended shortcuts (or matching tags): ;email, ;phone, ;firstname, ;lastname, ;fullname, ;address, ;city, ;zip, ;company, ;title

Placeholders

  • {{date}} – current date
  • {{time}} – current time
  • {{datetime}} – date + time
  • {{year}} – current year
